# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the OwLLM community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the maintainers via [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/OwLLM/owllm/discussions) (private message a maintainer if it's sensitive). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers may temporarily or permanently exclude any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful — including in Issues, Discussions, pull requests, or any direct communication on behalf of the project.
